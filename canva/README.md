# Sage & Stitch – Cart, Checkout aur Order Confirmed (Canva)

Yeh Sage & Stitch online clothing store ke design mein mera hissa hai, jo maine Canva mein Microx Software House ke team project ke liye banaya. Maine shopping ke aakhri teen steps design kiye hain: Cart, Checkout aur Order Confirmed.

**Designer:** Amta Hafeez
**Tool:** Canva
**Canva link:** https://canva.link/t0yzu2yn3h8dvl9

## Mere pages

**1. Cart**
Isme customer ke chune hue items nazar aate hain, unki image, size, colour, quantity aur price ke saath. Right side par Order Summary hai jisme subtotal, delivery, total, promo code ka box aur "Proceed to Checkout" button hai.

**2. Checkout**
Ek simple form hai jisme name, phone number, address, city aur postal code likhna hota hai. Customer payment method chunta hai (Cash on Delivery ya Credit/Debit Card). Right side par "Your Order" box mein products aur final total hai, aur "Place Order" button hai.

**3. Order Confirmed**
Ek thank-you page hai jisme order number, estimated delivery date aur status timeline (Order placed, Packed, Shipped, Delivered) hai. Isme product thumbnails, total, aur "Track Order" aur "Continue Shopping" buttons bhi hain.

## Team review ke baad maine kya theek kiya

- **Har jagah same total:** Order Confirmed par Rs. 4,580 tha jabke Cart aur Checkout par Rs. 7,398 tha. Maine teeno pages par Rs. 7,398 kar diya.
- **Checkout mein sahi products:** Maine "Sky Blue Printed Dress" ko "Sage Floral Midi Dress" se aur "Nourishing Face Oil" ko "Navy Wrap Dress" se badla, matching images aur prices ke saath.
- **Order Confirmed mein thumbnails:** khali boxes mein ab dono dresses nazar aati hain.
- **Cart par naya footer:** maine purane Legal links, 10% banner aur Subscribe button hata diye. Ab har page par ek hi footer hai jisme sirf ek copyright line hai.
- **Common header:** header mein ab New in, Women, Men, Home, Beauty, Sale hai, aur bag icon ke saath "Add to Cart" ki jagah "Cart" likha hai.
- **Ek hi price format:** tamam prices "Rs. 3,299" format mein likhi hain.

## Design 

- **Colours:** header aur footer ke liye sage green, halke neutral backgrounds, aur main buttons ke liye coral colour taake woh numayan nazar aayein.
- **Fonts:** headings aur product names ke liye serif font taake fashion store ka ehsas aaye, aur chhote labels ke liye simple sans-serif font.
- **Layout:** har page par same header aur footer hai, aur main content beech mein hai, taake sab pages ek hi website lagein.
- **Flow:** Cart, phir Checkout, phir Order Confirmed, aur har page par ek clear button agle step par le jata hai.

## Limitations

- Yeh static designs hain, is liye buttons aur form kaam nahi karte. Flow sirf visually dikhaya gaya hai.
- Customer ki sample details aur order number farzi hain.
